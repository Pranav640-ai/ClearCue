How to use this playground

  1. Enable ClearCue on this site from the side panel.
  2. Walk a workflow with your screen reader and note where you lose context.
  3. Compare what you experienced with the documented defect list at the bottom of each page.
  4. Use Reset this page to return the workflow to its starting state.

All names, amounts and documents are invented. Nothing here is a real service or a real application.
