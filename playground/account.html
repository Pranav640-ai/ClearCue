Intentional accessibility defects on this page (3)
  1. D-10 Unnamed switch. The email-notifications switch has the correct role and state but no accessible name.
  2. D-11 Ambiguous destructive action. “Delete account” uses the same primary styling as “Save changes” and sits immediately beside it.
  3. D-12 Silent confirmation. Saving shows a small check icon with no text, live region or status message.

Before you delete

  • Bookings are cancelled immediately.
  • Receipts remain available for 30 days.
  • This cannot be undone.
