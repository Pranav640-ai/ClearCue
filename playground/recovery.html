Stage 5 verification

Interaction recovery lab

These workflows reproduce outcomes that are visible but poorly communicated: silent validation, visual confirmations and ambiguous field errors. ClearCue observes the page; it never fills, submits, purchases or deletes anything for you.

Open the ClearCue side panel from the toolbar icon or Alt+Shift+C, enable this site, then use a workflow. Automatic announcements appear only when the page does not already expose the update.

Flagship workflow

Scholarship registration: 12-step recovery sequence

  1. Enter a registration number.
  2. Submit the form.
  3. A visual error appears without an announcement.
  4. ClearCue detects the new error.
  5. ClearCue announces it once through a page live region.
  6. Choose Jump to affected field.
  7. Correct the number.
  8. Submit again.
  9. A visual success panel replaces the form.
  10. ClearCue communicates the success.
  11. The panel keeps a local history.
  12. Turn on the accessible comparison to confirm the page's own announcement is not duplicated.

Use the registration number from your invitation email. This demonstration sends no application.

    There is a problem

    Changes saved.

    The delete-account control is intentionally absent from this lab. ClearCue must never activate a destructive action on your behalf.

    Intentional Stage 5 defects in this lab
    1. Silent inline validation and summary: no role=alert or aria-live, and focus stays on the submit button.
    2. Visual-only success panel: no live region and focus is not moved.
    3. aria-hidden shopping toast: visible confirmation that assistive technology cannot reach.
    4. Ambiguous account summary: two fields could be responsible; the safe behaviour is no field association.
    5. Accessible comparison adds real live regions and a focus move, so ClearCue must not duplicate its announcements.
